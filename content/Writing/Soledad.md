It was given to me "Jefferson Jeffri". Mom chose it for my older brother, so dad got the chance to choose mine.  I wasn't really conscious about it at first, as with many things. But my classmates started calling me like a football player from there "Jefferson Farfán", and that really annoyed me. 

I grew, and it was fine. We eventually left our country; no one knew who Farfán was. With time I learnt people could call me "Jeff" and that is when I started liking it a little. No one really cared about it, really, but I did. Jefferson was fine, but Jeffri? way too much. I should have been grateful, really. We, non-english speakers, sometimes butcher them. Jeffri could have easily been Yefri... Jefferson, Yeferson. The thought frightened me. Nonetheless, Jeffri was not Jeffrey, and, alas, I would always have that scar. 

I asked my mom about it, a couple of times. "Ask your dad" she said, but I never did. It kept following me for a while, and I felt for it. "This, is the only thing that is really mine. This is my dad's gift" I thought. And, as we moved, I started leaving it behind. It is weird seeing it on paper now, writing it. I remember how happy I was as a kid, writing my full name, it was so hard after all!
